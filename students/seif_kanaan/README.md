# Canadian Disasters Monitor

**Student:** Seif Kanaan
**Course:** OSS4008A — Introduction to Remote Sensing and Applications

## Project objective

Use satellite imagery to investigate changes associated with selected
Canadian wildfires, floods, earthquakes and tornadoes, and present the
results through interactive maps.

## Data and methods

- Sentinel-2 optical imagery for wildfire and vegetation-change analysis.
- Sentinel-1 radar imagery for flood and earthquake-related landscape-change analysis.
- Before-and-after comparisons, observation-quality masks and change indicators.

Results are exploratory satellite observations and do not establish
confirmed disaster damage or independently validated hazard boundaries.
